      Questions

      Personal training in South Mumbai.

      Which areas of South Mumbai do you cover?

      Sanjeev trains clients across South Mumbai, including Colaba, Cuffe Parade, Nariman Point, Churchgate, Marine Drive, Fort, Malabar Hill, Walkeshwar, Napean Sea Road, Breach Candy, Peddar Road, Kemps Corner, Tardeo, Mahalaxmi and Worli.

      Where do the sessions take place?

      Wherever suits you: at your home, your building's fitness centre, or the gym where you are already a member. Home sessions need a professional gym setup, and your gym needs to allow outside trainers.

      Do you offer online coaching?

      No. Every session is in person, one-on-one, with Sanjeev in the room.

      What does a home gym need for personal training?

      At minimum a power rack or squat stand, a barbell and plates, an adjustable bench and a set of dumbbells. Sanjeev can advise on equipment before you buy.

      How do I book a trial session?

      Pick a day and time in the booking section and confirm on WhatsApp or Calendly. Sanjeev confirms every booking personally.
