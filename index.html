      Questions

      Personal training in Mumbai & online.

      Which areas of Mumbai do you cover?

      All of Mumbai. Sanjeev trains clients in person anywhere in the city, at their gym or their home. Outside Mumbai or outside India, you can train with him online from anywhere in the world.

      Where do the sessions take place?

      Wherever suits you: at your home, your building's fitness centre, the gym where you are already a member, or online over a live video call. Home sessions need a professional gym setup, and your gym needs to allow outside trainers.

      Do you offer online training?

      Yes. Sanjeev trains clients all over the world with live one-on-one sessions over video call. Wherever you live, in India or abroad, he watches every rep, corrects your form in real time and plans each session around the equipment you have. Session times are arranged around your time zone.

      I live outside India. Can I train with Sanjeev?

      Yes. International clients train with Sanjeev online. When you book, the website shows each slot in your local time as well as India time, and if none of the listed times suit your time zone, message him on WhatsApp and he will find one that does.

      What does a home gym need for personal training?

      At minimum a power rack or squat stand, a barbell and plates, an adjustable bench and a set of dumbbells. Sanjeev can advise on equipment before you buy.

      How do I book a trial session?

      Pick a day and time in the booking section and confirm on WhatsApp or Calendly. Sanjeev confirms every booking personally.
